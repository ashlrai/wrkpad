# Community code of conduct

wrkpad contributors are expected to be respectful, technically honest, and welcoming. Debate designs and evidence without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or pressure maintainers to weaken safety and attribution boundaries.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the project maintainers or GitHub's private reporting tools. Good-faith security research and evidence-backed disagreement are welcome.
