# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, curious, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for newcomers and different communication styles.
- Respect privacy, consent, and security boundaries.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.
- Report harmful behavior privately when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate misinformation
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Attempts to bypass security, consent, or project governance
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful or inconsistent with this code.

Report conduct concerns to [hello@ashlr.ai](mailto:hello@ashlr.ai). Include links or screenshots when safe. Reports will be reviewed privately, shared only with people needed to resolve the issue, and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Enforcement decisions may consider severity, intent, impact, prior behavior, repair, and community safety.
